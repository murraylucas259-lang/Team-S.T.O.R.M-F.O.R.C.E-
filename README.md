# Team-S.T.O.R.M-F.O.R.C.E-
Team STORM FORCE stands for Severe Thunderstorm Observation &amp; Response Mission Field Operations, Reconnaissance, Control &amp; Evaluation. We are a group focused on weather and trying to warn people through YouTube, and look to expand on our knowledge of thunderstorms, severe weather environment. However we are small and not degreed in meteorology.
